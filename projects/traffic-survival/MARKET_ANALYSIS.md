## 6. Platform Constraints (Yandex Games)

### 6.1 Technical Constraints

| Constraint | Impact | Mitigation |
|-----------|--------|------------|
| Web-only (no native app) | Performance ceiling, no offline | Phaser 3 optimization, localStorage caching |
| Yandex Games SDK only | No third-party ad networks | Use ysdk.adv exclusively |
| Landscape 2400×1080 | Mobile-first design | Responsive scaling via Config |
| No cloud saves (initially) | Data loss on clear cache | localStorage + future Yandex cloud sync |
| 19K games in catalog | High competition | Quality content, niche positioning |
| Quality bar rising | Thinning content apps risk removal | Must have real gameplay, not just text viewer |

### 6.2 Moderation Risks

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|------|----------|------------|
| Thin content removal | HIGH | Quiz mechanic = real gameplay, not just joke viewer |
| Black humor content moderation | MEDIUM | Age gate 18+, avoid offensive categories |
| Low engagement = removal | HIGH | Retention systems (daily rewards, streaks, progression) |
| Ad fraud detection | HIGH | Never place ads during active gameplay taps |

### 6.3 SDK Requirements

- `ysdk.adv.showFullscreenAdv()` — interstitials
- `ysdk.adv.showRewardedVideo()` — rewarded ads
- `ysdk.features.LoadingAPI.ready()` — signal game ready
- `ysdk.getLeaderboards()` — global leaderboard (P2)
- `ysdk.getPlayer()` — player data sync (P2)
- **Mandatory:** Ad monetization integration required for publication

### 6.4 Opportunity Areas

1. **40% international traffic:** Localization to EN could 2x addressable audience
2. **Hybrid monetization trend:** IAP for premium joke packs, no-ads pass
3. **LiveOps potential:** Seasonal joke packs, themed events

## 8. Key Findings & Recommendations

### 8.1 Market Viability: **FAVORABLE**

- Yandex Games is growing (50M+ MAU, +10% YoY)
- Quiz genre has strong retention potential (D1 ~50% for top trivia)
- No direct competitor in joke-guessing niche
- Ad eCPM supports viable revenue at scale

### 8.2 Critical Success Factors

1. **Content volume:** 100+ jokes at launch is minimum viable (competitors: 600-200K)
2. **First 10 minutes:** Must deliver 3-5 jokes with satisfying feedback loop
3. **Daily return hooks:** Daily rewards + streaks are non-negotiable for D7 retention
4. **Interstitial frequency:** Every 4 jokes is optimal; every joke = churn risk
5. **Rewarded ads:** Must be meaningful (+100 coins = 10 correct answers worth)
