## Executive Summary

**Market Thesis:** The browser fighting game market is **wide open with no dominant player**. The fighting game genre is experiencing a renaissance — $1.58B–$4.8B market in 2026 growing at 7.2–8.7% CAGR — driven by free-to-play models, roguelite hybrids (PolyFighter, Shot One Fighters), and esports expansion. On browser portals specifically, all top fighters use generic stickman art with basic mechanics. The roguelite + fighter mashup is validated on PC but **zero exist on browser portals**. Poki has 100M+ MAU, CrazyGames has 50M+ MAU. 46% of online consumers play browser games monthly. The window is open but closing — ship fast, establish portal presence, win.

## 3. Trends

### Fighting Game Trends (2026)
1. **Roguelite + Fighter mashup validated** — PolyFighter (PAX/EVO 2026, Kotaku "favorite game"), Shot One Fighters ($221K Kickstarter, Justin Wong), Garden Souls (TGA nominee dev)
2. **Free-to-play dominance** — 2XKO (Riot) proving F2P + cosmetic model; F2P holds 61.5% of global gaming market ($180.6B)
3. **Rollback netcode standard** — eliminated #1 barrier for online fighting games
4. **Tag team / multiplayer expansion** — 2026 releases focus on 2v2/3v3/4v4
5. **Mobile fighting fastest growing** — 10.2% CAGR, faster than console (6.8%) and PC (7.8%)
6. **Indie breakout** — Rivals of Aether II tripled EVO entrants (363→1022)

### Browser Gaming Trends (2026)
1. **Mid-Core is the new gold mine** — session duration quadrupled from 5 min to 25+ min for top games; CrazyGames avg session now 30 minutes
2. **53% of mobile devs plan browser ports** in next 12 months — supply flood incoming
3. **Web gaming is mainstream** — 46% of online consumers play browser games monthly
4. **Mobile browser dominance** — 62–81% of traffic is mobile; touch-first mandatory
5. **Consented inventory premium** — rewarded video earns 2× unconsented eCPM
6. **Established mobile IPs coming to web** — SYBO, Fingersoft, Outfit7 partnering with Poki
7. **Google H5 Games Ads reopened** — first-party ad option for web developers

### Mobile Gaming Trends (2026)
1. **Revenue up, downloads down** — $81.8B IAP in 2025 (+1.3%), but downloads declined 7.2% to 50.4B
2. **Hybridcasual rising** — revenue up 20% to $4.2B in 2025
3. **Strategy dominates revenue** — 4X strategy titles (Last War, Whiteout Survival) driving growth
4. **Retention declining** — median D1 dropped to 22%, D7 to 4%, D30 to 0.7–0.8%
5. **Top 1% concentration** — 92.5% of IAP revenue from top 1% of publishers
6. **Ad monetization shifting** — rewarded video drives 62% of mobile game ad revenue

### Browser Fighting Game Specific
1. **Stickman dominance continues** — 90%+ of competitors use generic stickman art = **#1 differentiation opportunity**
2. **Online multiplayer gap** — only ONE game (Gladihoppers) offers online PvP on Poki
3. **No dominant browser fighter exists** — all top fighters have basic mechanics
4. **Ragdoll physics trending** — Ragdoll Hit has 591K+ votes on Poki

## 8. Recommendations Summary

1. **Ship fast** (MVP: 8–10 weeks) — distinctive art, 4+ characters, local 2P, touch controls, roguelite gauntlet
2. **Target Poki primary** (100M+ MAU, best revenue) **+ CrazyGames secondary** (50M+ MAU, fast approval)
3. **Design for ≤8MB initial load** — critical Poki requirement; aggressive sprite optimization
4. **Touch-first from day one** — 62–81% of traffic is mobile; virtual joystick + 48px buttons
5. **Monetize via rewarded video** — $4–$8 blended eCPM take-home; target $0.08–$0.15 ARPDAU
6. **Roguelite progression** — unique on portals; "one more try" loop drives retention and ad impressions
7. **Add online multiplayer Phase 2** — Poki Netlib P2P, zero server costs, massive differentiation
8. **Distinctive non-stickman art** — #1 visual gap; 90%+ competitors use stickmen; portal thumbnail standing
9. **Target 5–12 minute sessions** — aligns with Poki 11–20 min benchmark; CrazyGames avg 30 min
10. **Plan for 12-month rolling average** — Q4 CPMs +40–70%, Q1 trough; don't budget on spot figures
