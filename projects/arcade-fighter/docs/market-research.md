### Demographics
- **Gender:** Historically male-dominated (~75–80%), but rapidly diversifying. Casual fighters and accessible indie titles are expanding female audience.
- **Geography:** Asia-Pacific (38–44%), North America (28–29%), Europe (19–20%), Latin America (8–9%), MENA (5%).
- **Platform Preference for Web Games:** Desktop browser (~47%), mobile browser (~62% of all browser game sessions, TFWH 2026). Mobile browser traffic dominates HTML5 gaming with 81% of total sessions (Business Research Insights 2026).
- **Session Length:** Successful Poki games achieve 11–20 minute average sessions (Poki 2026 State of Web Gaming). CrazyGames data from 1.74B US play sessions (May 2025–May 2026) shows average session is now 30 minutes (CrazyGames/Google Analytics, May 2026). Weekday traffic is 22% higher than weekends. Browser fighters should target 5–15 minute sessions.

### Player Behavior (Poki 2026 Study — 2,000 players surveyed by Atomik Research)
- **37% of gamers play web games "multiple times per day"** — deeply engaged audience.
- **86% play at least a few times per week** — high-frequency habit.
- **27% spend $50+/month** on purchases tied to a free-to-play browser title — console-grade ARPU.
- **62% have downloaded or purchased a full game after discovering it via browser** — web is the top-of-funnel (72% among most frequent players).
- **92% of players describe HTML5 games as "quite" or "very" high quality** — quality perception is strong.
- **58% choose web games because they are free and frictionless** — zero-download is the primary draw.
- **28% stopped playing a mobile app because the download size was too large** — friction is real.
- **90% of web gamers multitask while playing** — integrated into daily routines.
- **Average session:** Most common web gamer plays 11–20 minutes per session, engaging with 2–3 titles.
- **Casual players spend $5–30/year per title** on cosmetics and battle passes.
- **71% of web gamers also own consoles** (42% PS4/5, 27% Xbox, 27% Switch) — web gaming is complementary, not replacement.
- **46% of online consumers ages 13–55 played a browser game in the past month** (Newzoo-Google Sept 2026, 7,500 sample).
- Fighting game audiences are highly streamable (1v1 clarity, dramatic comebacks) — strong Twitch/YouTube appeal.
- The FGC (Fighting Game Community) is grassroots-driven, highly social, and brand-loyal.

## 3. Market Trends (2025–2026)

### Fighting Game Industry Trends
1. **Rollback Netcode Standard:** Industry-wide adoption of rollback netcode (GGPO-derived) has eliminated the #1 barrier to online fighting games. Essential for any multiplayer browser fighter.
2. **Tag Team / Multiplayer Expansion:** 2026's biggest releases (Marvel Tōkon, 2XKO, Invincible VS) are 2v2/3v3/4v4 tag fighters — the genre is moving beyond strict 1v1.
3. **Free-to-Play Dominance:** 2XKO (Riot Games) is proving F2P + cosmetic monetization works at scale in fighting games. F2P segment holds 61.5% of the global gaming market ($180.6B in 2025).
4. **Seasonal Content Cycles:** Season passes with regular character drops (every 1–3 months) are the dominant post-launch model.
5. **Accessibility Revolution:** Modern fighters (SF6, etc.) use simplified controls, modern control schemes, and AI-assisted training to lower the skill floor.
6. **Indie Breakout:** Rivals of Aether II tripled its EVO entrants (363→1022) in one year, proving indie fighters can compete with AAA.
7. **Cross-Platform Play:** Expected by players; critical for browser fighters to connect with console/PC communities.
8. **Streaming & Spectator Focus:** 1v1 clarity makes fighting games highly streamable; browser fighters can leverage this for organic discovery.
9. **Mobile Fighting Games Fastest Growing:** Mobile fighting game segment growing at 10.2% CAGR — faster than console (6.8%) and PC (7.8%).
10. **Roguelite + Fighter Mashup Validated (2026):**
    - **PolyFighter** (HeartLoop Games) — Confirmed Steam EA October 13, 2026. Showcased at PAX East 2026, PAX West 2026, and EVO 2026. 40+ unlockable characters, move-stealing roguelike progression. Kotaku called it "far and away my favorite game I played at PAX East." Steam demo live.
    - **Shot One Fighters** (Red Moon Workshop) — 2.5D roguelite fighter with FGC veteran Justin Wong and JMCrofts collaboration. 100+ moves, 100+ artifacts, multiple acts. Official selection at Day of the Devs Summer Game Fest 2026. Kickstarter planned.
    - **Garden Souls** (Statera Studio) — Action roguelite with fighting-game cancel system, 4 distinct characters, Steam playtest live (September 2026). Developer previously nominated for TGA 2023 Best Fighting Game (Pocket Bravery).
    - **Roguelite Games Market:** $4.8B in 2025, projected to reach $11.6B by 2034 at 10.3% CAGR (Dataintelo). Steam hosts 2,400+ roguelite titles. Genre driven by procedural generation, AI-assisted content, and streaming virality.

### Web/Browser Gaming Trends (2026 Verified)
1. **Rewarded Video is King:** Highest eCPM format for web games. US web rewarded eCPM: $4–$15 net take-home after splits. Blended global web eCPM: $3.62–$6.98 (AppLixir 2026). Opt-in rate: 97%. Completion rate: 93.8% global, 95.4% tier-1.
2. **Multi-Portal Distribution:** Developers publish on itch.io + Poki/CrazyGames + own site simultaneously. Non-exclusive deals are standard.
3. **Revenue Share Models (2026 verified):**
   - Poki: 50/50 split (100% on own traffic); web exclusive deals preferred (5-year term); non-exclusive available (one-time flat fee). Top studios earning up to €1M/year (Poki/Dealroom 2026). 625M players visited in 2025.
   - CrazyGames: 60% ads / 70% IAP (per 2026 jam terms); €100 payout minimum, NET 60. 60M+ monthly players, ~$25M+ annual revenue.
   - Playgama Bridge: 80% developer share (Cinevva 2026 compilation)
   - itch.io: Developer sets cut (default 10%)
   - GameMonetize: ~45%
   - GameDistribution: Syndicated to 4,000+ portals, 350M monthly reach
4. **Phaser as Dominant Framework:** Phaser 3/4 is the most popular 2D game framework for browser development. Proven by Grapplenauts, Artemis Ninja Hunter, DAWPUNCH, Stickforge.
5. **AI-Assisted Development:** Tools can scaffold fighting game prototypes rapidly.
6. **Mobile Browser Priority:** 62–81% of web game traffic is mobile; responsive/touch-friendly design is mandatory.
7. **Instant Play Advantage:** Zero-download experience is the core value prop vs. app stores.
8. **Mobile-to-Browser Migration:** 53% of mobile developers plan to port to browser in next 12 months — supply explosion coming.
9. **Quality Perception Shift:** 92% of players rate HTML5 games as high quality; 32% of developers still perceive web as "low quality" — perception gap exists.
10. **Consented Inventory Premium:** Under ATT and Privacy Sandbox, unconsented traffic earns 20–40% lower eCPMs. Rewarded video is opt-in by design, earning premium rates.
11. **WebGL/In-Canvas Ad Resistance:** WebGL and in-canvas ad formats resist ad blockers — structural advantage for browser games.
12. **PWA Adoption Growing:** Progressive Web App adoption in gaming up 63%, reducing load times to under 2.5 seconds.
13. **Median Portal Revenue:** Well-performing casual titles land in $200–$2,000/month range; top Poki studios reach up to €1M/year (Poki/Dealroom). Distribution is brutally uneven — median outcomes matter for planning. CrazyGames analysis: 1.74B US play sessions (May 2025–May 2026), 862M hours of playtime. Hypercasual rules browser (79% of year). Weekday traffic 22% higher than weekends.

### Browser Fighting Game Trends (September 2026)
1. **Stickman Dominance Continues:** Almost every browser fighter uses stickman art. This is the #1 differentiation opportunity.
2. **Ragdoll Physics Trending:** Ragdoll Hit has 591K+ votes on Poki — the most popular mechanic in browser fighters.
3. **Online Multiplayer Gap:** Only ONE game offers online multiplayer on Poki (Gladihoppers) — massive gap.
4. **Multiple Phaser+Colyseus Fighters in Dev:** Burki Fighter, Riz-Fighters, SteamPunch, Grapplenauts — pipeline is growing.

7. **Garden Souls Playtest Live (Sep 2026):** Statera Studio (Pocket Bravery, TGA 2023 nominee) opened free Steam playtest. Uses fighting-game cancel system in a roguelite structure. Validates roguelite + fighter mashup at the highest quality level.

### Phaser 3 Constraints & Considerations
1. **Performance:** Phaser 3 handles 2D arcade fighters well. Arcade Physics is sufficient for hitbox/hurtbox collision detection.
2. **Mobile:** Current config uses `Phaser.Scale.FIT` with `CENTER_BOTH` — good. Touch input must be added for mobile fighters (virtual joystick/buttons).
3. **Networking:** For multiplayer, Phaser pairs well with Colyseus (WebSocket server) or Socket.io. Same JS/TS stack on client and server. Grapplenauts uses Bun runtime for better server performance.
4. **Asset Loading:** Phaser's asset loader supports spritesheets, atlas, audio — sufficient for fighting game needs.
5. **Scene Management:** Already implemented (Boot → Preloader → MainMenu → GameScene) — solid foundation.
6. **Build:** Webpack setup is production-ready. Output is a static HTML/JS/CSS bundle — ideal for portal upload.
7. **Physics:** Matter.js integrated with Phaser can handle complex physics. Stickforge validates stickman joint physics at scale.
8. **WebGL Advantage:** WebGL and in-canvas ad formats resist ad blockers — structural advantage for monetization.

|---|---|---|

## 7. Recommendations

### Game Design Direction
1. **Session Length:** Target 5–12 minute sessions (matches of 60–90 seconds each). Aligns with Poki's 11–20 minute session benchmark.
2. **Accessibility:** Implement simplified controls (2–3 buttons + directional) with optional advanced inputs for depth.
3. **Game Modes:** Start with local 2-player (shared keyboard) + vs CPU. Add online multiplayer as Phase 2.
4. **Visual Style:** Pixel art or stylized 2D — low asset cost, strong retro appeal, performant on mobile browsers.
5. **Progression:** Roguelite structure or arcade ladder with unlockable characters/cosmetics.
6. **Distinctive Art:** NOT stickman. This is the #1 differentiation opportunity — 90%+ of competitors use stickmen.

### Monetization Path
1. **Phase 1 (MVP):** Free-to-play on Poki/CrazyGames with rewarded video ads (character trials, extra continues, cosmetic unlocks). Target: $0.08–$0.15 revenue per DAU (action/arcade benchmark).
2. **Phase 2 (Growth):** Add cosmetic IAP store (skins, effects, stages) — target after 10K+ daily active players.
3. **Phase 3 (Scale):** Season Pass / Battle Pass system with regular character drops.

### Distribution Strategy
1. **Primary:** Poki (100M+ MAU, largest audience, best revenue for quality games)
2. **Secondary:** CrazyGames (50M+ MAU, fast approval, action category, non-exclusive)
3. **Community:** itch.io (direct sales, feedback, community building)
4. **Discovery:** Newgrounds (retro audience, art community)

### Technical Priorities
1. Add touch controls for mobile browser support (mandatory for 62–81% of traffic)
2. Implement responsive scaling for various screen sizes
3. Design for ≤8MB initial load (Poki requirement) / ≤50MB (CrazyGames)
4. Build input system that supports keyboard + touch from day one
5. Target < 3 second load time on 4G
