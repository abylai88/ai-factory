## Executive Summary

**Market Thesis:** The browser fighting game market is **wide open with no dominant player**. The fighting game genre is experiencing a renaissance — $1.76B–$4.8B market in 2026 growing at 4.2–8.7% CAGR — driven by free-to-play models, roguelite hybrids (PolyFighter, Shot One Fighters), and esports expansion. On browser portals specifically, all top fighters use generic stickman art with basic mechanics. The roguelite + fighter mashup is validated on PC but **zero exist on browser portals**. Poki has 100M+ MAU, CrazyGames has 50–60M MAU. 46% of online consumers play browser games monthly. The window is open but closing — 53% of mobile devs plan browser ports in next 12 months. Ship fast, establish portal presence, win.

**Key Update (Sep 2026):** 2XKO (Riot Games) announced wind-down of active development at end of 2026, removing battle pass and ranked play. This confirms that F2P + cosmetic monetization alone may not sustain fighting games — validating Battle Brawl's ad-first + cosmetic hybrid approach for browser. Meanwhile, roguelikes generated nearly $500M on Steam in H1 2026 alone (Alinea Analytics), proving the genre's commercial power.
