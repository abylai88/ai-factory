## 4. Market Trends (2025–2026)

### Trending Now (verified from platform data and Q2 2026 earnings)

1. **Retention Over Monetization (CRITICAL)**
   - Roblox RFY algorithm now evaluates player behavior over 28-day window (up from 7 days)
   - Impact: games with high D7/D30 retention get more impressions, even if per-hour monetization drops
   - Q2 2026 impact was "greater than anticipated" on younger cohorts
   - Implication: **Design for retention loops first, monetize second**

2. **Viral Engagement Spikes**
   - Steal An Egg reached 1.2M CCU in just 1 month
   - Grow a Garden peaked at 462K+ CCU, normalized to 25-33K
   - 28% of top 100 games by hours were launched in preceding 90 days
   - Implication: **Build sticky core loop that retains beyond viral phase**

3. **Return Frequency > Session Length**
   - Core users now have 6–8 sessions/day (33% YoY increase)
   - Games need "re-entry hooks" — offline progression, timed events, daily rewards
   - Implication: **Design for 3–5 daily returns per user**

4. **18+ Audience Explosion**
   - 27% of age-checked DAU are 18+, growing 50%+ YoY (18-34 cohort)
   - This cohort spends 40–50% more than under-18
   - Roblox offering 42% DevEx rate increase for 18+ US spend in eligible games
   - Implication: **Design content that appeals to 13–25 age range; use R15 avatars for 18+ DevEx eligibility**

5. **Friend-to-Friend Co-play Drives Discovery**
   - Algorithm rewards intentional co-play
   - Group play = stickier sessions + organic growth
   - Implication: **Build team modes, party systems, friend leaderboards**

6. **Incremental/Idle Loops Proven at Scale**
   - Grow a Garden: 35.9B visits with "garden grows while offline" mechanic
   - Pet Simulator 99: 2.6B visits with layered prestige loops
   - +1 Speed Monkey Escape: 497M visits in 3 months with speed incremental loop
   - Key: stacked design with collection, upgrade, prestige, trading

7. **Trading Economy = Longevity Signal**
   - Active trading hubs are the single best signal of long-term staying power
   - Dead trading hubs indicate games that have peaked
   - Implication: **Design tradeable items with meaningful scarcity**

8. **Creator Rewards Program (since July 2025)**
   - **Daily Engagement:** 5 Robux per Active Spender (spent $9.99+ in 60 days) who plays 10+ min in one of first 3 daily games
   - **Audience Expansion:** 35% revenue share on first $100 spend by new/reactivated users (60-day window)
   - Replaced engagement-based payouts and Creator Affiliates
   - This is baseline passive income before any player spending

9. **Cross-Experience Passes Disabled (May 2026)**
   - Cannot sell game passes across experiences
   - Must monetize within your game only
   - Use Robux transfers for any cross-game mechanics

   - Roblox discovery now prioritizes D1/D7/D30 retention (28-day window)
   - Design session length: 8–15 minutes (mobile-friendly)
   - Implement "re-entry hooks" — offline progress, timed events

   - 18+ cohort growing 50%+ YoY, spending 40-50% more

   - Enable Roblox's dynamic regional pricing

7. **Content Rating: Aim for "Mild" or "Moderate"**

   - U13 accounts cannot see rewarded ads — design accordingly

8. **Creator Rewards + Audience Expansion Optimization**
